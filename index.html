What This Movement Is FROM THE FOUNDER

Heal Loudly Movement is for any trauma or grief you are facing — you do not have to go through it alone. I have navigated the darkness myself, through many different kinds of trauma. I am experienced in pain, loss, and grieving. You are not alone.

I'm raising up a movement to stand with me and take an active approach to healing — to rebel against shrinking away to the world's expectations, just to make them comfortable around your pain.

I encourage you to take baby steps — only what you can do, at your own pace. No pushing. No fear. Do what you can do, and nothing more.

This app is a non-judgmental, safe, private place to speak anything you like, feel anything you need to, and process your emotions. You will only find encouragement here.

I keep God at the center of all I do. I am Christian — but I welcome with open arms anyone who is hurting. I do not push my beliefs on anyone.

This app and my book are built on themes of healing, love, forgiveness, and creating beauty from the wreckage of the storm. ⛈️🌟

— The Founder, Heal Loudly Movement 🦁
